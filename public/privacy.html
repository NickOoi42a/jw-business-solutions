Privacy notice

This notice explains how information submitted through this website’s enquiry form is handled.

What you share

When you submit an enquiry, you provide your name, email address, optional company name, chosen service, message, and permission for us to respond. Please avoid including sensitive personal information.

How it is used

We use these details to review your enquiry, respond to you, and discuss possible services. Submitting an enquiry does not subscribe you to a marketing mailing list.

How it is delivered

The website and backend are hosted on Vercel. Enquiries are passed through JW Consulting’s existing Vercel email service and delivered by Resend to nickooi42a123@gmail.com, hosted by Gmail. Your email is included as the reply-to address. These providers may process data outside your country. Google Fonts supplies the typefaces and receives ordinary browser connection information when fonts load.

Retention and requests

Enquiries are retained in our email inbox as needed to manage the conversation and business records. You can request access, correction, or deletion of your enquiry by emailing nickooi42a123@gmail.com.

Technical information

The form uses validation and a hidden spam field. The website does not add analytics or advertising trackers. Hosting and email providers may process technical request logs for operation and security.
